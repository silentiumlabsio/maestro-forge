# Failure writeup worksheet

Status: template; no incident or student result is claimed.

## Intended outcome
What should have happened, with a concrete success criterion?

## Reproduction
Exact repository/commit, runtime, public-safe input, steps, expected result and observed result. If not reproduced, say so.

## Scope and impact
Who or what was affected? Avoid names, private data and blame. Security-sensitive incidents belong in private reporting.

## Cause and evidence
Separate confirmed causes from hypotheses. What competing explanation was checked?

## Correction
Smallest useful change, regression check, observed outcome and remaining limitations.

## Learning
Which assumption changed? What signal would catch this earlier? Credit collaborators with consent.
