# Maestro Forge Community

Maestro Forge is a student-focused open-source ecosystem created and maintained by Silentium Labs. It is an independent community project for Maestro students and is not an official Maestro product or repository.

Community participation includes building, reviewing, documenting, designing, testing, mentoring, experimenting, sharing resources, and proposing ideas.

Executable student projects remain in contributor-owned repositories by default. Maestro Forge curates metadata, links, evidence, reviews, and approved educational content.
