# Code of Conduct

Maestro Forge is a learning community. Be constructive, specific, and respectful.

- Critique code, not people.
- Help beginners without talking down to them.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Do not submit malware, credential stealers, destructive payloads, harassment content, or intentionally deceptive code.
- Do not publish another student's private information.
- Do not abuse issues or pull requests for spam or promotion.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the project.
