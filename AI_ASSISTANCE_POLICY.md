# AI Assistance Policy

Maestro Forge is AI-friendly and skill-first.

## Allowed

Contributors may use AI tools for:

- brainstorming;
- explaining concepts;
- generating starter code;
- debugging;
- writing tests;
- documentation assistance;
- refactoring suggestions.

## Required responsibility

The contributor owns the submission and must:

- understand the submitted behavior;
- review generated dependencies and commands;
- remove secrets and unsafe code;
- disclose material AI assistance in the PR checklist;
- complete Skill Verification for a verified score.

## Not allowed

- submitting code the contributor refuses or is unable to explain during verification;
- using AI to fabricate test output, screenshots, benchmark results, citations, or completion evidence;
- hiding malware, credential collection, unauthorized scanning, persistence, destructive behavior, or data exfiltration behind generated code;
- falsely claiming someone else's work as original.

## No AI detection scoring

Maestro Forge does not use AI-text or AI-code detectors to assign guilt or reduce scores. Those systems are not reliable enough for fair competition.

Fairness comes from code-specific verification, adaptation, debugging, transparent rubrics, and reviewer evidence.
