# Academic Integrity

Maestro Forge exists to help students learn by building, not to host answer keys.

Do not upload:

- restricted exams or quizzes;
- copied graded assignment solutions when redistribution is prohibited;
- proprietary course PDFs, videos, slides, question banks, or instructor material without permission;
- plagiarized third-party work.

Projects inspired by course concepts are welcome when they are original and do not expose restricted course content.
