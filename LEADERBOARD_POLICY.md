# Leaderboard Policy

The leaderboard is designed to reward demonstrated skill and contribution, not volume spam.

## Separate signals

Display separate values instead of one opaque popularity score:

- verified challenge score;
- completed Skill Verified challenges;
- accepted project contributions;
- documentation/review contributions;
- challenge authoring contributions.

## Skill ranking

Only Skill Verified challenge results are eligible for score-based ranking.

Do not rank unverified submissions by stars, likes, follower count, AI disclosure, or number of commits.

## Anti-gaming

- repeated trivial PRs do not multiply prestige;
- duplicate/copied solutions can be rejected;
- reviewers may request individual verification;
- team members verify individually for individual ranking;
- maintainers may remove fraudulent scores with documented rationale.
